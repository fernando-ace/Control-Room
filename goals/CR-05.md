# CR-05: Second cooperative mission

## Objective and context

Add a genuinely different cooperative scenario using the integrated multi-mission foundation. It must require Commander, Pilot, and Engineer to share information and coordinate, rather than reskinning Solar Storm. Inspect the architecture and existing game flow first.

## Dependencies

CR-01. Integrate the architecture goal before implementing this mission.

## Invariants

Keep exactly three players and the established role/privacy model unless product requirements explicitly change later. Server-authoritative timestamps, outcomes, CAS/revision persistence, deduplication, reconnect, retry, and outsider isolation remain intact. Solar Storm remains unchanged.

## Scope

Design and implement one distinct scenario with differentiated role knowledge/objectives, server-owned rules, clear briefing, and deterministic tests. Reuse the mission selection seam.

## Non-goals

Do not rewrite Solar Storm, add more than one new mission, introduce external services, or weaken multiplayer checks.

## Definition of done

- The new scenario is selectable and completable/defeatable through real server-side rules.
- All three roles have necessary, complementary information and no role receives another role's private data.
- Tests cover timing, actions, outcomes, retries, and role-filtered snapshots.
- Solar Storm regression coverage remains intact.

## Validation and completion

Inspect implementation and tests before coding. Run lint, typecheck, all unit/API tests, production build, relevant live multiplayer/browser validation, and `git diff --check`. Never weaken assertions to pass. Report incomplete if criteria are not met. Review and commit locally; do not push or deploy.
